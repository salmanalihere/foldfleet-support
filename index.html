About Foldfleet

Foldfleet is a naval strategy game designed specifically for iPhone Duo. Deploy your fleet, search for enemy ships, and use strategy and deduction to sink the opposing fleet.

Play against the AI or challenge another player with pass-and-play multiplayer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Foldfleet?

Place your fleet on your grid, then take turns targeting positions on the opposing grid. Use your previous hits and misses to determine where enemy ships may be hiding.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes. Foldfleet includes AI gameplay for single-player naval strategy battles.

Can two people play together?

Yes. Foldfleet supports pass-and-play multiplayer on the same device.

Is Foldfleet designed for iPhone Duo?

Yes. Foldfleet is designed specifically around the iPhone Duo experience.
